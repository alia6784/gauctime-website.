Our Story

Guac Time brings fresh, bold, customizable Mexican-style food to Bay Ridge. Founded in 2021, the brand is known for burritos, bowls, tacos, fries, and guac made for fast, satisfying meals. The Bay Ridge location stands out as a reliable late-night spot for people who want big portions, fast service, and strong flavor.
